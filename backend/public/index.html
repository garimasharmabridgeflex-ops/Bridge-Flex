KFlex

Staffing for nurseries and early-years practitioners

Download Android APK Admin Console
For practitioners: Browse and accept shifts, log ratings, submit timesheets, view payroll profiles and earnings.
For nursery admins: Manage staff scheduling, approve shift applicants, run vetting checklists, assign nursery plans, generate invoices and payroll periods, view audit logs.
Distribution: Direct APK download — the app is not on Google Play. Side-load the APK on Android devices.
