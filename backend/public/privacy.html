Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Bridge Flex mobile app and service collect, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and what rights you have over it.

Bridge Flex is a staffing marketplace that connects childcare settings (nurseries, pre-schools and similar) with early-years staff for individual shifts. It is intended for use by adults acting in a professional capacity.

1. Who we are

Bridge Flex Recruitment is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights in section 9, contact us at afrilexkenya@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

Account data

  • Email address and a password, or — if you choose Sign in with Google — your Google account's email address, name and profile photo.
  • Your role on the platform (childcare setting or staff member).
  • Name, phone number, and an optional profile photo.

If you register as a staff member

  • Professional details: years of experience, qualification level and qualifications, skills, languages spoken, a short bio or professional summary, and previous roles.
  • Availability: which days and shift types you can work, and how far you are willing to travel.
  • Age, city, and nationality.
  • Right-to-work and visa status.
  • DBS certificate number and expiry date, plus any document images you upload for verification.

If you register as a childcare setting

  • Setting name, registered company name, and Ofsted registration number and self-reported rating.
  • Address, postcode, contact email and phone number, website, year established, setting type, opening hours, facilities, and photos or a logo you upload.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device's location to match you with nearby shifts. Your precise coordinates are stored privately and are never shown to other users — other users only ever see an approximate area derived from them.

Documents you upload

Images of DBS certificates and similar documents are stored securely and are reviewed by a Bridge Flex administrator to verify your status. They are not visible to other users of the platform.

Activity on the platform

  • Shifts you post, accept, or cancel, and attendance outcomes such as no-shows.
  • Chat messages exchanged with the other party to a booked shift.
  • Ratings and written reviews you give or receive after a shift.

Device and technical data

  • A push notification token, so we can notify you about shifts, messages and ratings.
  • App version and basic usage and diagnostic data collected via Google Analytics for Firebase (for example screens opened and app crashes), used to understand how the app is used and to fix faults.

We do not collect data about children. Bridge Flex is a tool for childcare professionals and setting managers. Shift listings describe age groups and numbers of children in a room, but never any individual child's identity or personal information.

4. What other users can see

The app deliberately separates your private record from what is shown to other users. Other users can see your name, role, profile photo, approximate area, rating, DBS verification badge, and the professional details relevant to booking (experience, qualifications, availability, bio, and — for settings — the setting's public details).

Other users cannot see your exact location coordinates, your phone number, a setting's exact street address, your DBS certificate number, or any document image you upload.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

  • Other users of the platform, limited to the public details described in section 4.
  • Google LLC / Google Cloud, which provides the authentication, database, file storage, notification, analytics and hosting infrastructure the service runs on, acting as our processor. See the Firebase privacy documentation.
  • Apple, where the app is distributed through TestFlight or the App Store, and for delivering push notifications to iOS devices.
  • Authorities or regulators, where we are legally required to disclose information.

6. Where your data is stored

Our database and backend services are hosted in Google Cloud's London (europe-west2) region. Some supporting services, such as authentication and push notification delivery, are operated by Google and Apple globally and may involve transfers outside the UK. Those transfers rely on the safeguards those providers offer, including the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data — while your account is active.
  • Shift, chat and rating records — retained after a shift completes, so both parties keep an accurate history and so ratings remain meaningful.
  • Verification documents — retained while needed for safeguarding verification.

You can delete your account from within the app at any time (Profile → Delete account). Deleting your account removes your profile and login credentials. Records of shifts you worked, and ratings exchanged with other users, may be retained where we have a legitimate or legal reason to keep them.

8. Security

Access to data is enforced on our servers, not just in the app: every request is authenticated, and database and file-storage rules restrict each record to the users entitled to see it. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our infrastructure provider. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased, where no overriding reason to keep it applies;
  • restrict or object to how we process your data;
  • receive your data in a portable form;
  • withdraw consent — for example by turning off location or notification permissions in your device settings at any time.

To make a request, email afrilexkenya@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and — where the change is significant — tell you in the app.
