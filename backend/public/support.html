KFlex Support

We aim to reply to every message within 2 working days.

KFlex connects childcare settings — nurseries, pre-schools and similar — with early-years staff for individual shifts. This page is where to get help with the app, your account, or anything that has gone wrong.

Contact us: afrilexkenya@gmail.com

Please include the email address on your KFlex account, whether you use the app as a setting or as a staff member, and your device (iPhone or Android). That lets us look into it without a round of back-and-forth.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time — you do not need to contact us, and there is no waiting period:

  • Open the Profile tab.
  • Tap Delete account in the Account & privacy section (it is also available under the settings gear icon).
  • Confirm when asked.

This permanently removes your profile, your uploaded documents, your public listing and your login. It cannot be undone. Records that are shared with other people — a shift a setting has already run, or a review someone else wrote — are kept as part of their history, with your profile no longer attached.

If you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes KFlex's access to your Apple ID as part of deletion.

I can't sign in

KFlex accepts an email and password, Sign in with Apple (on iPhone and iPad), and Sign in with Google. These are separate logins: if you created your account with Google, use the Google button rather than a password. If you forgot your password, use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen to have a reset link emailed to you.

I used Sign in with Apple and my name is blank

Apple only shares your name with an app the very first time you authorise it. If it did not come through, open Profile → Edit profile and set your name there.

My DBS or document is still pending

Uploaded documents are checked by a person, not automatically. If yours has been pending for more than 3 working days, email us and we will look at it.

A shift I applied for hasn't been confirmed

Applying to a shift does not book it. The setting reviews applicants and accepts or declines each one, and the shift stays visible to other staff until it is fully staffed. You will get a notification either way.

How do I report a problem with another user?

Email afrilexkenya@gmail.com with the person's name and the shift or conversation involved. We investigate every report and can suspend accounts.

How do I get a copy of my data, or have it corrected?

See section 9 of the Privacy Policy, or email us — we respond to access, correction and erasure requests within one month.

Reporting a bug

If something in the app is broken, it helps enormously if you tell us:

  • What you tapped, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • The screen it happened on.
  • Your device and app version — the version is shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.
