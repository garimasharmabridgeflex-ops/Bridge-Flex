1. What KFlex is

KFlex is a platform that introduces childcare settings (nurseries, pre-schools and similar) to early-years staff who are available to work individual shifts. Settings post shifts; staff apply; the setting decides who to accept.

The working arrangement for any shift is between the setting and the staff member. KFlex is not a party to it. We do not supervise shifts, set pay rates, or direct how work is carried out.

2. Who may use KFlex

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must be using KFlex in a professional capacity — either on behalf of a childcare setting you are authorised to represent, or as a person seeking early-years work.
  • You must have the legal right to work in the United Kingdom in order to accept shifts.
  • You must not create an account if you are barred from working with children, or if a previous KFlex account of yours was suspended or removed.

3. Your account

You may sign in with an email address and password, with Apple, or with Google. You are responsible for keeping access to your account secure, and for everything done through it. Tell us promptly at afrilexkenya@gmail.com if you believe someone else has used your account.

Everything you put on your profile must be accurate and about you — your name, qualifications, experience, DBS details and right-to-work status included. One person or setting, one account. Do not impersonate anyone or let another person use your account.

4. If you are a staff member

  • Only claim qualifications, training and experience you actually hold, and upload only genuine documents that belong to you.
  • Keep your DBS certificate and right-to-work status current, and update KFlex when they change. A setting is entitled to rely on what your profile says.
  • Turn up to shifts you have been accepted for, on time. If you cannot make a shift, cancel in the app as early as you reasonably can so the setting can re-fill it.
  • Follow the setting's policies and safeguarding procedures while you are on their premises.

5. If you are a childcare setting

  • Describe shifts honestly — the role, hours, location, pay rate and anything a staff member needs to know before accepting.
  • Carry out your own statutory checks. KFlex shows you what a staff member has told us and what documents we have seen, but it does not replace your own safer-recruitment, DBS, right-to-work, ratio and induction obligations under the EYFS and applicable law.
  • Provide a safe working environment, and pay agreed rates for work done.
  • Decide on applicants within a reasonable time so staff are not left waiting.

6. Shifts, applications and cancellations

Posting a shift is an invitation for staff to apply. Applying to a shift does not book it — a shift is only booked when the setting accepts that applicant, and it remains open to other applicants until the number of staff it requires has been accepted.

Either side may cancel a booked shift through the app. Repeated late cancellations or non-attendance may result in suspension under section 11.

7. Pay

Pay for a shift is agreed between the setting and the staff member at the rate shown on the shift. Unless we tell you otherwise in the app, payment is made directly by the setting; KFlex does not hold, process or guarantee it. Tax, National Insurance and any employment-status obligations arising from the work are a matter for the setting and the staff member.
